Privacy Policy

At SnapCompress, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors.

Local Processing

SnapCompress operates entirely client-side using JavaScript HTML5 Canvas. Your uploaded images are never transferred to, stored on, or processed by external servers. All compression happens locally inside your web browser.

Cookies and Third-Party Ads

We may use third-party advertising companies like Google AdSense to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.
